Abstract

The Myntra Sales Analysis project is a complete end-to-end data analytics solution built as an internship assignment. It analyses a synthetic Myntra-style e-commerce sales dataset of 500 transaction records across 17 attributes, covering 8 Indian cities over a 6-month period (January–June 2026). The project pipeline includes data cleaning, exploratory data analysis, 10 professional visualisation charts, and an interactive Streamlit web dashboard — delivering actionable business insights on sales performance, product popularity, customer behaviour, city-wise trends, payment preferences, and profitability.

Data Cleaning (data_cleaning.py)

The cleaning script validates and transforms the raw Excel dataset before any analysis. The original file is never modified. Cleaning steps performed:

  1. Load: pd.read_excel('Myntra_Sales_Dataset.xlsx', sheet_name='Myntra Sales Data') → 500 rows × 17 columns confirmed
  2. Missing Values: df.isnull().sum() → No missing values found ✅
  3. Duplicates: df.duplicated().sum() → 0 duplicate records ✅
  4. Date Conversion: Order Date converted to datetime → all 500 dates parsed successfully ✅
  5. Derived Columns: Year, Month, Month_Name, Month_Year extracted from Order Date
  6. Numeric Enforcement: 8 columns cast to float64 (Quantity, Unit Price, Discount %, Discount Amount, Rating, Sales, Cost, Profit) → 0 coercion errors ✅
  7. Categorical Standardisation: 7 columns (City, Customer Type, Gender, Product, Category, Payment Method, Order Status) whitespace-stripped
  8. Range Validation: Negative Sales: 0 | Negative Profit: 0 | Invalid Discount%: 0 | Out-of-range Rating: 0 ✅
  9. Output: Saved to data/cleaned/cleaned_myntra_sales.csv with 21 columns

Key Findings & Business Insights

Note: All findings are based on the synthetic dataset. They do not represent actual Myntra operations.

  • Top Revenue Category: Identified from chart 01 — the leading category drives the largest share of total sales revenue across all 8 categories
  • Top Products: High-value items (Watches, Sports Shoes, Sneakers, Sarees) purchased in multiple units (Qty 2–4) dominate the Top 10 by Sales chart
  • City Performance: All 8 Indian cities contribute across the 6-month period; metro cities with high-value order concentrations lead total sales
  • Customer Type: Both New and Returning customers are present; Returning customers on established platforms typically drive repeat revenue
  • Payment Method: UPI is the most frequently used payment method, consistent with India's digital payments growth
  • Order Fulfilment: Majority of orders are Delivered; Return and Cancellation rates are tracked and visualised in chart 09
  • Monthly Trends: Sales and profit variation across Jan–Jun 2026 reveals seasonal patterns; peak months identified from charts 06 and 07
  • Discount Correlation: The scatter chart (Figure 10) with Pearson r quantifies whether discounts drive proportional sales uplift
  • Average Rating: Customer ratings range 3.0–5.0; average ~4.0 indicates positive satisfaction in the synthetic dataset

Business Recommendations (Based on Synthetic Dataset)

  1. Inventory Planning: Prioritise top-revenue categories for stock allocation and promotional budget
  2. Discount Strategy: Avoid blanket discounting if correlation is weak; use targeted discounts on slow-moving SKUs
  3. Customer Loyalty: Invest in loyalty programmes for returning customers to maximise lifetime value
  4. Payment Optimisation: Offer UPI-specific cashback to reduce cart abandonment and improve conversion
  5. City-Specific Campaigns: Increase ad spend in top-performing cities; run awareness campaigns in lower-performing ones
  6. Returns Reduction: Investigate high return-rate product categories to improve product descriptions and sizing accuracy
  7. Seasonal Planning: Pre-stock inventory before identified peak months to avoid stockouts

Limitations

  • Synthetic Dataset: 500 synthetic records — cannot be generalised to actual Myntra operations
  • Limited Size: 500 records is insufficient for statistically robust pattern detection
  • Short Period: 6 months (Jan–Jun 2026) — inadequate for long-term trend or year-on-year analysis
  • Limited Variables: No inventory levels, marketing spend, competitor data, or customer demographics
  • Static Data: Dashboard reads a static CSV — no live database or real-time streaming
